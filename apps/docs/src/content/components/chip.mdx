## Purpose

Chips show short pieces of information such as a status, a tag or an active filter. They can be
static, clickable (`onClick`) or removable (`onDelete`). Do not use chips as the primary way to trigger
actions; use buttons for that.

## Accessibility

- Clickable and deletable chips are focusable. <kbd>Enter</kbd> activates; <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or
  <kbd>Delete</kbd> triggers `onDelete`.
- Static chips are not focusable and are read as text.
- Do not rely on color alone for status: include the status in the label.
