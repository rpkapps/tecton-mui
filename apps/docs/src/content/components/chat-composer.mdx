## Purpose

`ChatComposer` is where the user writes: an auto-growing text area, the attachment list, helper text
and a toolbar with the attach and send buttons. `ChatBox` includes one; use `ChatComposer` yourself
inside a `ChatProvider` when you build your own layout, and `useChatComposer()` to read or drive the
draft from any component in the chat.

## Building a composer from its parts

Without children, `ChatComposer` renders its default parts. Pass children to choose them:
`ChatComposerAttachmentList`, `ChatComposerTextArea`, `ChatComposerHelperText` and a
`ChatComposerToolbar` with `ChatComposerAttachButton` and `ChatComposerSendButton`. Give the buttons
a Tecton `Icon` and an `aria-label`. `variant="compact"` puts everything on one row, and
`features={{ attachments: false }}` turns attachments off.

The text area grows with its content up to `maxRows`. Pressing <kbd>Enter</kbd> sends, and
pressing <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> adds a line break. A running input method (IME)
composition is never sent by accident.

## Driving the draft with a hook

`useChatComposer()` returns the draft `value`, `setValue`, the `attachments` with `addAttachment`
and `removeAttachment`, `clear`, `submit` and `isSubmitting`. Here, buttons insert report templates,
a counter checks the length, and a custom button sends.

## Disabled composer

`disabled` (on `ChatComposer`, or `slotProps.composerRoot.disabled` in a `ChatBox`) blocks typing
and sending, for example in a read-only channel. Say why in the placeholder or next to the chat.

## Accessibility

- The text area is labelled "Message" (`localeText.composerInputAriaLabel`), and the composer is a
  landmark named "Message composer" (`localeText.composerLandmarkLabel`).
- The send button is disabled until there is text or a file, and while an answer streams.
- Icon-only buttons need an `aria-label`; the default ones have one.
