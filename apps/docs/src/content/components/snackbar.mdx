## Purpose

Snackbars confirm an operation briefly at the edge of the screen and disappear on their own. They may
offer one action, typically "Undo". Show one snackbar at a time.

## Accessibility

- The content has `role="alert"`, so screen readers announce the message when it appears.
- Give users enough time: keep `autoHideDuration` at 5 seconds or more, and longer when there is an
  action. The timer pauses while the snackbar is hovered or focused and when the window loses focus.
- Do not put essential information or the only path to an action in a snackbar.
- `SnackbarContent` accepts the Tecton `type` prop (`success` or `error`) when used on its own.
