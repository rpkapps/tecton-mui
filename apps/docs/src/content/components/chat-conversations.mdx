## Purpose

A chat with several threads has a conversation list beside the open thread, and a header above the
thread with its title, subtitle and actions. In a `ChatBox`, turn the list on with
`features={{ conversationList: true }}`; on its own, `ChatConversationList` and `ChatConversation`
work inside a `ChatProvider`. The [Chat page](/components/chat#conversations) shows loading
conversations and their history from the adapter.

## List items

Each item shows the avatar, the `title`, a preview line (the `subtitle` by default), the time of
`lastMessageAt` and a badge for `unreadCount`. `variant="compact"` makes the rows denser. Replace the
parts with `slots` on the list (in a `ChatBox`: `slotProps.conversationList.slots`): `itemAvatar`,
`title`, `preview`, `timestamp`, `unreadBadge`, `itemActions`, or the whole `item`. Put data of your
own in the conversation's `metadata`. Here the preview shows each well's status.

## Header actions

The header shows the open conversation's `title` and `subtitle`. `slots.conversationHeaderActions`
fills the actions area on its trailing side; the slot renders inside the chat, so `useChat()` gives
the open conversation. Here the actions pin and mute conversations, which the application owns as
controlled `conversations`.

## Unread counts and read receipts

`unreadCount` draws the badge, and `features.unreadMarker` marks the first unread message in the
thread. When the user opens a conversation, clear its count in your state and report it with
`adapter.markRead`, so the backend can update read receipts for everyone else. The chat does not call
`markRead` on its own.

## Accessibility

- The list is a `listbox` named "Conversations", with one `option` per conversation;
  `aria-selected` marks the open one. Arrow keys move through it and <kbd>Enter</kbd> or
  <kbd>Space</kbd> opens a conversation.
- The header is a `header` element with the conversation title as text. Give icon buttons in the
  actions area an `aria-label`, and `aria-pressed` when they toggle.
- Below 600px the list becomes a dialog opened from a menu button in the header; focus moves into
  it and back.
