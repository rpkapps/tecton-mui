## Purpose

Avatars represent people, teams or systems with a photo, initials or an icon. `AvatarGroup` stacks
several avatars and summarises the rest as "+N".

## Basic usage

Tecton sizes are `xsmall` (18px), `small` (24px), `medium` (32px) and `large` (40px). Avatars with
initials get a stable accent color derived from their content.

## Accessibility

- An avatar image needs `alt` text (`<Avatar alt="Ada Lovelace" src="…" />`).
- Initials alone are often meaningless to screen-reader users; show the name next to the avatar or add
  an `aria-label`.
- The "+N" surplus avatar in a group is text; mention who is hidden elsewhere if that matters.
