## Purpose

Image lists display a collection of images in a grid, for example thumbnails of seismic sections,
core photos or site imagery. Choose the `variant` (`standard`, `quilted`, `woven` or `masonry`) that
suits the shape of the images.

## Parts

- `ImageList` lays out its items in `cols` columns with `rowHeight` and `gap`.
- `ImageListItem` holds one image; `cols` and `rows` let it span several cells.
- `ImageListItemBar` adds a title, subtitle and actions over or below the image.

Always give each image a meaningful `alt` text, or an empty `alt` when it is decorative.
