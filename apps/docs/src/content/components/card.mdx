## Purpose

Cards group content and actions about one subject, such as a project, a dataset or a metric. They work
well in grids and dashboards. Avoid nesting cards and avoid putting many unrelated actions on one card.

## Anatomy

`CardHeader` (avatar, title, actions), `CardContent` and `CardActions` give consistent spacing.

## Accessibility

- A card is a plain container. Use headings inside it (`CardHeader` with `slotProps.title` set to a
  heading component, or `Typography component="h3"`) so cards appear in the document outline.
- To make the whole card clickable, wrap its content in `CardActionArea`; do not nest other buttons
  inside the action area.
