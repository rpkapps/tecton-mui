## Purpose

Steppers show where the user is in a numbered sequence, such as a multi-step form or a set-up
wizard, and what is left. Use them for three to seven steps; for one or two use a single form, and
for navigation between unrelated views use [Tabs](/components/tabs).

## Parts

- `Stepper` holds the steps and sets `activeStep`, the `orientation` and whether the flow is
  `nonLinear`.
- `Step` is one step; `completed`, `disabled` and `active` are derived from `activeStep` unless set.
- `StepLabel` renders the step number or icon with its title and optional text (for example
  "Optional" or an error message).
- `StepButton` replaces `StepLabel` in non-linear steppers so each step can be selected.
- `StepContent` holds the content of a step in a vertical stepper and animates it open.
- `StepIcon` and `StepConnector` are the default icon and connecting line; replace them with
  `slots.stepIcon` on `StepLabel` and the `connector` prop on `Stepper`.

For a compact stepper on small screens, see [Mobile Stepper](/components/mobile-stepper).
