## Purpose

The calendar components render a picker view inline, without a field or popup: `DateCalendar` for days,
`MonthCalendar` for months and `YearCalendar` for years. Use them in side panels, filters or when the
calendar is the main content.

## Validation and limits

`minDate` and `maxDate` bound the navigation; `shouldDisableDate` disables single days. Disabled days
stay visible and are announced as unavailable. `shouldDisableMonth` and `shouldDisableYear` work the
same way on the other views.

## Month and year calendars

`MonthCalendar` and `YearCalendar` are standalone grids of months and years. Both edit a full `Date`,
so they can share one value.

## Views

`views` and `openTo` turn `DateCalendar` into a year → month → day flow, useful for dates far from
today such as commissioning or birth dates.

## Loading data

Set `loading` while the data behind a month (bookings, reports, shifts) is fetched. `renderLoading`
replaces the day grid; `DayCalendarSkeleton` keeps the layout stable.

<Demo name="LoadingCalendar" />

A typical pattern starts a request in `onMonthChange`, cancels the previous one and sets `loading`
until the data for the new month has arrived. The booked days are then marked through
`slotProps.day`, memoized so the calendar only re-renders when the data changes.

<Demo name="AsyncBookingsCalendar" />

The same props work on every picker with a day view, so the popup of a `DatePicker` shows
`DayCalendarSkeleton` while its month is loading.

<Demo name="LoadingDatePicker" />

## Localization and week numbers

The adapter locale sets month and weekday names and the first day of the week. `displayWeekNumber`
adds a column of week numbers; `fixedWeekNumber` keeps the height constant between months.

## Accessibility

- `DateCalendar` is a grid: arrow keys move by day and week, <kbd>Page Up</kbd>/<kbd>Page Down</kbd>
  by month, <kbd>Home</kbd>/<kbd>End</kbd> to the start/end of the week.
- Today's date is marked visually and announced. Disabled dates are announced as unavailable.
- Inline calendars have no field label: name them with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, or place them
  in a labelled region.

## Limitations

- Range selection on an inline calendar is provided by `DateRangeCalendar` on the
  [range pickers](/components/date-range-pickers) page.
