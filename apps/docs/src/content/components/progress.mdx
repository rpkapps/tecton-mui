## Purpose

Progress indicators show that the application is working. Use a **determinate** indicator when the
amount of work is known and an **indeterminate** one otherwise. Prefer skeletons for content that is
loading into a known layout.

## Accessibility

- Indicators have `role="progressbar"`; determinate ones expose `aria-valuenow`. Name every indicator
  with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- Mark the region being updated with `aria-busy="true"` while loading, and announce completion with
  visible text.
- Animations respect the user's reduced-motion preference where upstream supports it.
- Tecton adds `secondary` and `tertiary` colors to both components.
