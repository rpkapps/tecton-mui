## Purpose

`SimpleTreeView` displays a hierarchy declared with JSX: each `TreeItem` has an `itemId`, a `label`
and nested items as children. It suits small and medium trees whose structure is known when rendering,
such as navigation or settings. For large or data-driven trees, use the
[Rich Tree View](/components/rich-tree-view).

## Multi-selection

`multiSelect` lets users select several items with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+click,

<kbd>Shift</kbd>+click and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrow keys. `defaultSelectedItems` sets the initial
selection.

## Checkbox selection

`checkboxSelection` adds a checkbox to every item. Control the state with `selectedItems` and
`onSelectedItemsChange`.

## Controlled expansion

`expandedItems` and `onExpandedItemsChange` let you expand or collapse items from outside the tree, for
example with "Expand all" buttons or to reveal a search result.

<Demo name="ExpansionControl" />

## Disabled items

A `disabled` item and its children cannot be selected or expanded. By default disabled items are
skipped by keyboard navigation; `disabledItemsFocusable` keeps them reachable so their label can be
read. Explain why an item is disabled in its label or nearby text.

## Custom icons

The `expandIcon`, `collapseIcon` and `endIcon` slots replace the default chevrons. Use Tecton
[icons](/components/icon) to show what an item is.

## Events and expansion trigger

`onItemClick`, `onItemFocus` and `onItemExpansionToggle` report interaction. With
`expansionTrigger="iconContainer"` only the icon expands an item, so clicking the label selects it
without toggling.

## Accessibility

- Follows the ARIA tree pattern: the view is a `tree`, items are `treeitem`s with `aria-expanded` and
  `aria-selected`. Name the tree with `aria-label`.
- Keyboard: <kbd>Up</kbd>/<kbd>Down</kbd> move between visible items; <kbd>Right</kbd> expands or moves
  to the first child; <kbd>Left</kbd> collapses or moves to the parent; <kbd>Home</kbd>/<kbd>End</kbd>
  jump to the first/last item; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> selects; typing a character moves
  to the next item starting with it; <kbd>\*</kbd> expands all siblings.
- In right-to-left layout the <kbd>Left</kbd>/<kbd>Right</kbd> behaviour is mirrored.

## Limitations

- Every item must have a unique `itemId`.
- Lazy loading, drag-and-drop reordering and label editing belong to the Rich Tree View.
