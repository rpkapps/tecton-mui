## Purpose

Clock components render time selection inline, without a field or popup. `TimeClock` is an analog
clock face, `DigitalClock` a single scrollable list of times, and `MultiSectionDigitalClock` one list
per section (hours, minutes, seconds, meridiem). They are the views the time pickers open, so
everything here also applies to `TimePicker` and `DateTimePicker`.

Clocks need a [`LocalizationProvider`](/guides/localization) above them, like every picker.

## Analog clock

Select the hour, then the minute: the clock moves to the next view after each choice. `ampmInClock`
places the AM/PM toggle inside the face instead of in a toolbar.

<Demo name="AnalogClock" />

## Views

`views` lists the sections the clock cycles through. Control the visible one with `view` and
`onViewChange`, for example from your own tabs or toggle buttons. `ampm={false}` uses a 24-hour face.

<Demo name="ClockViews" />

## Digital clock

`DigitalClock` lists times at a fixed interval (`timeStep`, in minutes). It stretches to the width of
its container, so place it in a sized element such as a `Paper` or a side panel. The selected option is
scrolled into view when the clock mounts.

<Demo name="DigitalClockList" />

## Multi-section digital clock

`MultiSectionDigitalClock` shows one column per section. `timeSteps` sets the interval of each
section, for example `{ minutes: 5 }`. Add `'seconds'` to `views` for precise timestamps.

<Demo name="MultiSectionClock" />

## Validation and limits

`minTime`, `maxTime` and `shouldDisableTime` restrict the options. `shouldDisableTime` receives the
candidate time and the view, so you can disable a whole hour or single minutes. With `skipDisabled`,
the digital clocks hide disabled options instead of greying them out.

<Demo name="ClockValidation" />

## Read-only and disabled

`readOnly` keeps the value visible and focusable but ignores changes; `disabled` also removes the clock
from the tab order.

## Localization

The adapter locale sets the time format: the 12/24-hour default, the meridiem labels and digits.
Override the 12/24-hour choice with `ampm`.

## Accessibility

- The digital clocks are listboxes: <kbd>Up</kbd>/<kbd>Down</kbd> move between options,
  <kbd>Home</kbd>/<kbd>End</kbd> jump to the first and last option and <kbd>Enter</kbd> selects. In
  the multi-section clock, <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves between sections.
- The analog clock is operable with the arrow keys and announces the selected hour and minute. For
  keyboard-heavy workflows the digital variants are usually faster.
- Inline clocks have no field label: name them with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, as in the
  demos above.

## Limitations

- `DigitalClock` and `MultiSectionDigitalClock` take the full width of their container (upstream
  behaviour). Give them a sized parent rather than placing them directly in a flex row.
- Time ranges are provided by the separate [range pickers](/components/date-range-pickers).
