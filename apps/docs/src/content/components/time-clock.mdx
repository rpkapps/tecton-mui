## Purpose

Clock components render time selection inline. `TimeClock` is an analog clock face, `DigitalClock` a
single scrollable list of times, and `MultiSectionDigitalClock` separate lists for hours, minutes and
the meridiem.

## Accessibility

- The digital clocks are listboxes: arrow keys move between options and <kbd>Enter</kbd> selects.
- The analog clock is operable with arrow keys and announces the selected hour and minute; for
  keyboard-heavy users the digital variants are usually faster.
