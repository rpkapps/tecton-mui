## Purpose

Accordions organise long content into sections that users expand on demand, such as settings groups or
FAQs. If users need to compare sections side by side, show the content directly instead.

## Accessibility

- The summary is a button with `aria-expanded`; give it `id` and `aria-controls` so it is linked to the
  region it controls.
- Keyboard: <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves between summaries; <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles.
- The heading level of the summary defaults to `h3`; set `slotProps.heading.component` to fit your
  outline.
- Collapsed content is hidden from assistive technology; do not hide required form fields in it.
