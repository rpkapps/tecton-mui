## Purpose

Drawers hold navigation or supplementary content that slides in from an edge of the screen. A
temporary drawer opens over the content and closes on selection or when the backdrop is clicked; a
persistent or permanent drawer sits beside the content.

## Parts

- `Drawer` renders the panel with a `variant` of `temporary` (a modal, the default), `persistent` or
  `permanent`, on the `anchor` edge you choose.
- `SwipeableDrawer` is a temporary drawer that can also be opened and closed with a swipe on touch
  devices. It needs both `onOpen` and `onClose`.
