## Purpose

An alert displays a short, important message in the page flow without blocking the user: the result
of an operation, a warning about data, an error that needs attention. For temporary confirmations use
a [Snackbar](/components/snackbar); for decisions that must be made before continuing use a
[Dialog](/components/dialog).

## Accessibility

- Alerts have `role="alert"`, which makes screen readers announce them **when they appear**. Render
  them in response to events; an alert that is present on page load may not be announced.
- Do not use alerts for content that is always visible and unimportant; frequent interruptions are
  disruptive.
- Write messages that say what happened and what to do next. Do not rely on the color alone.
- Tecton's design gives alerts a fixed width of 320px (the default `size="medium"`). Place them where
  that width fits, for example in a side panel or above a form.
