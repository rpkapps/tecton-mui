## Purpose

The heading sits at the top of a page, under the app bar: the page's title, the tabs that switch
between its sections and the page's own actions, such as creating an item or changing the view.

`Heading` is the Tecton page heading from the Figma design (the "Organisms / Heading" stories of the
Tecton Storybook). Material UI has no such component, so it is composed from Material parts, and
you compose it from its own parts, MUI style:

| Part             | What it is                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Heading`        | The row, with the Figma frame's padding                                 |
| `HeadingTitle`   | The title (an `<h1>`), with optional home and menu buttons              |
| `HeadingTabs`    | MUI `Tabs` props for MUI `Tab` children, as the Tecton filled tab group |
| `HeadingActions` | Your buttons, selects and toggles, at the end of the row                |

## Basic heading

A title and one action.

<Demo name="BasicHeading" />

The title is a `Typography` with the Tecton heading 1 type (the theme's `h4` variant), rendered as
an `<h1>`, since a page has one main heading. `Heading` itself is a `<div>`; `component="header"`
makes it a `<header>`.

## Home and menu buttons

`onHomeClick` shows a home button before the title; `onMenuClick` shows a chevron after it, for a
menu that switches to another page of the same kind. The buttons take `IconButton` props through
`slotProps.homeButton` and `slotProps.menuButton`: an `aria-label` (by default "Home" and "More"),
the `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` of the menu the chevron opens, or an `href`
that makes the home button a link (it then shows without `onHomeClick`).

<Demo name="TitleButtonsHeading" />

## Tabs

`HeadingTabs` takes the MUI `Tabs` props (`value`, `onChange`, `size`, `aria-label`...) and holds
MUI `Tab` elements. It always shows the Tecton filled tab group (`Tabs` with `variant="pills"`) on a
paper surface, so the props that only apply to other variants or to vertical tabs are not offered.
Your page owns `value` and updates it in `onChange`, as with `Tabs`.

## Actions

`HeadingActions` sits at the end of the row and spaces its content: icon buttons, buttons, selects
and toggle button groups. Separate groups of actions with a vertical
`<Divider orientation="vertical" flexItem emphasis="subtle" />`. A select can carry its label
inside, before the value, as a start adornment.

<Demo name="ActionsHeading" />

## Page heading

The full heading: title, tabs and actions. When they do not fit in one row, the row wraps instead of
overflowing the page: the actions move to a line of their own, still at the end, then the tabs. At
very narrow widths the tab group wraps its tabs onto more rows. Make the window narrower to see it.

## Section headings and gutters

`Heading` has the Figma frame's padding: `space-400` (32px) above and below and `space-900` (72px)
at the sides. Inside a container that has its own padding, `disableGutters` removes the side padding,
and `sx` adjusts the rest (`sx={{ px: { xs: 2, md: 9 } }}` for smaller gutters on small screens). For a
heading below the page's main one, give the title another element and variant through
`slotProps.title`, for example `{ component: 'h2', variant: 'h5' }`.

## Customization

Each part takes `sx` and `className`. The parts carry the stable classes of `headingClasses`
(`TectonHeading-root`, `-title`, `-titleText`, `-homeButton`, `-menuButton`, `-tabs`, `-actions`),
for styles that reach into the heading from outside.

## Accessibility

- The title is a heading element (`<h1>` by default), so screen reader users can jump to it. Use
  `<h2>` and below for headings of page sections.
- The home and menu buttons are icon buttons with an accessible name ("Home" and "More" by default).
  Give the menu button a name that says what it switches, such as "Switch portfolio", and the
  `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` of its menu.
- The tabs are an MUI tab list: Tab reaches it once (on the selected tab), Left and Right arrow move
  between tabs, Home and End jump to the first and last, Enter or Space selects. Give
  `HeadingTabs` an `aria-label`, and each `Tab` an `id` and `aria-controls` pointing to its panel.
- Icon buttons in the actions need an `aria-label`; a select whose label is an adornment needs one
  too (`inputProps={{ 'aria-label': 'Sort' }}`).
- The row reflows down to 320px without horizontal scrolling (WCAG 1.4.10).
