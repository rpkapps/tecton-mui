## Purpose

The heading sits at the top of a page, under the app bar: the page's title, the tabs that switch
between its sections and the page's own actions, such as creating an item or changing the view.

`Heading` is the Tecton page heading from the Figma design (the "Organisms / Heading" stories of the
Tecton Storybook). Material UI has no such component, so it is composed from Material parts, and
you compose it from its own parts, MUI style:

| Part             | What it is                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Heading`        | The row, with the Figma frame's padding                                 |
| `HeadingTitle`   | The title (an `<h1>`), with optional home and menu buttons              |
| `HeadingTabs`    | MUI `Tabs` props for MUI `Tab` children, as the Tecton filled tab group |
| `HeadingActions` | Your buttons, selects and toggles, at the end of the row                |
| `HeadingSearch`  | A Search button that opens into a search field, among the actions       |

The title is a `Typography` with the Tecton heading 1 type (the theme's `h4` variant), rendered as
an `<h1>`, since a page has one main heading. `Heading` itself is a `<div>`; `component="header"`
makes it a `<header>`.

## Home and menu buttons

`onHomeClick` shows a home button before the title; `onMenuClick` shows a chevron after it, for a
menu that switches to another page of the same kind. The buttons take `IconButton` props through
`slotProps.homeButton` and `slotProps.menuButton`: an `aria-label` (by default "Home" and "Page
menu"), the `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` of the menu the chevron opens (it is a menu button,
`aria-haspopup="menu"`), or an `href` that makes the home button a link (it then shows without
`onHomeClick`).

## Tabs

`HeadingTabs` takes the MUI `Tabs` props (`value`, `onChange`, `size`, `aria-label`...) and holds
MUI `Tab` elements. It always shows the Tecton filled tab group (`Tabs` with `variant="pills"`) on a
paper surface, so the props that only apply to other variants or to vertical tabs are not offered.
Your page owns `value` and updates it in `onChange`, as with `Tabs`. When the heading runs out of
room, the last tabs move into the menu of a More tab at the end of the tab list (see
[Responsive layout](#responsive-layout)); a tab picked there is selected through the same
`onChange`.

## Actions

`HeadingActions` sits at the end of the row and spaces its content, MUI components: icon buttons
(search, filter), selects, a toggle button group (the page view), buttons, and the primary action, a
contained button, last. Separate groups of actions with a vertical
`<Divider orientation="vertical" flexItem emphasis="subtle" />`. A select can carry its label
inside, before the value, as a start adornment. The heading gives its controls the Figma heading's
look: 32px buttons and selects, the select's value right after its label, the view toggle's glyph
and label 6px apart, icon glyphs in the tertiary text color.

When the heading runs out of room, the actions fold into an overflow (⋮) menu before the primary
action, which never folds (see [Responsive layout](#responsive-layout)). The menu lists them as menu
items, read from the components themselves: an icon button is an item with its icon and its
`aria-label`, a button an item with its label, a select its label and its options as radio items, a
toggle button group its `aria-label` and its buttons as radio items. A pick runs the action: the
button's `onClick`, or the select's or the group's `onChange` with the picked value, as if it were
picked in the row.

## Search

`HeadingSearch` is a Search button that opens into a search field in place, among the actions
(usually first). Like a `TextField`, it takes `value` and `onChange(event, value)`, and your page
filters what it shows. Type below.

<Demo name="SearchHeading" />

- The field grows out of the button toward the start of the row, and what sits before it moves over
  to make room (it simply appears when the system asks for reduced motion). The heading measures
  the field at its full size first, so any action that must collapse does so before it grows.
- The field opens focused. Escape clears it; on an empty field, Escape closes it and gives the
  focus back to the button. The clear button empties it too.
- The field only closes empty: with a search in progress it stays open when the focus leaves it, so
  the page never filters by a term nobody can see.
- `label` names the button, the field and its search landmark (default "Search"); `placeholder`
  defaults to it. `size` (`medium` by default, or `small`) sizes the button and the field like the
  buttons and inputs beside them. `open`, `onOpen` and `onClose(event, reason)` control the field from outside, and
  `slotProps.button`, `slotProps.input` (a `FilledInput`) and `slotProps.clearButton` reach its
  parts.
- In a narrow heading, a closed search folds into the overflow menu after the other actions, as a
  Search item that opens it back in the row. An open field keeps its place in the row: the other
  actions and the tabs make room for it.

## Responsive layout

The heading behaves as the Tecton drop-in heading (`<wc-heading>`) does. Above 720px (its own width,
not the window's) it keeps one row; when its parts do not fit, they give way one step at a time:

1. **Actions.** They fold into an overflow (⋮) menu, which comes right before the primary action:
   first the buttons (a secondary action), then the toggle button groups (the view toggle), the
   selects, the icon buttons (filter before search) and a closed `HeadingSearch`; an open one keeps
   its place. The primary action never folds.
2. **Tabs.** Then the last tabs move into a More tab at the end of the tab list, down to the More
   tab alone. Its menu lists them; while the open tab is among them, the More tab is the selected
   one.
3. **Title.** A title that still does not fit ends with an ellipsis. The actions still keep the
   space between two actions (`space-200`, `space-100` on a narrow heading) from the tabs or the
   title: an open search field, which never folds, shortens the title rather than touch the tabs.

At 720px and below the heading is **narrow**: `space-200` (16px) all round, the tabs one dropdown
(the More tab alone, named after the open tab) on a row of their own, the primary action an icon
button named by its label, and the actions folding as before.

The heading measures its parts again whenever its own width or a part's width changes (a label, a
font that loaded, a tab added), before the browser paints, so nothing flickers. Drag the slider to
see it.

## Section headings and gutters

`Heading` has the Figma frame's padding: `space-400` (32px) above and below and `space-900` (72px)
at the sides, and a narrow heading (720px and below) `space-200` (16px) all round. The heading's own
width counts, not the window's, so a heading in a narrow panel is laid out as on a phone. Inside a container that has its own padding, `disableGutters` removes the
side padding, and `sx` adjusts the rest. For a heading below the page's main one, give the title
another element and variant through `slotProps.title`, for example
`{ component: 'h2', variant: 'h5' }`.

## Customization

Each part takes `sx` and `className`. The parts carry the stable classes of `headingClasses`
(`TectonHeading-root`, `-compact`, `-title`, `-titleText`, `-homeButton`, `-menuButton`, `-tabs`,
`-tabGroup`, `-moreTab`, `-actions`, `-overflowButton`, `-overflowMenu`, `-search`,
`-searchButton`, `-searchField`), for styles that reach into the heading from outside. The More tab
and its menu take props through `HeadingTabs`' `slotProps.moreTab` and `slotProps.moreMenu` (for
example a translated label), the overflow button and its menu through `HeadingActions`'
`slotProps.overflowButton` and `slotProps.overflowMenu`.

## Accessibility

- The title is a heading element (`<h1>` by default), so screen reader users can jump to it. Use
  `<h2>` and below for headings of page sections.
- The home and menu buttons are icon buttons with an accessible name ("Home" and "Page menu" by
  default). Give the menu button a name that says what it switches, such as "Switch portfolio", and
  the `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` of its menu.
- The tabs are an MUI tab list: Tab reaches it once (on the selected tab), Left and Right arrow move
  between tabs, Home and End jump to the first and last, Enter or Space selects. Give
  `HeadingTabs` an `aria-label`, and each `Tab` an `id` and `aria-controls` pointing to its panel.
- Icon buttons in the actions need an `aria-label`; a select whose label is an adornment needs one
  too (`inputProps={{ 'aria-label': 'Sort' }}`).
- The tabs and actions that do not fit are left out of the page, not hidden: the tab list holds only
  the tabs it shows, and the keyboard never lands on something out of sight. The More tab and the
  overflow button reach them.
- The More tab is a tab in the tab list and a menu button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`,
  `aria-expanded`): its menu lists the hidden tabs. After a pick, and on Escape, the focus goes back
  to it.
- The overflow button ("More actions") is a menu button too. In its menu, an option of a select or
  of a toggle button group is a radio item (`menuitemradio`, `aria-checked`) under the control's
  name. An action that folds while it has the focus leaves it to the overflow button.
- The open search is a search landmark (`role="search"`) holding a labelled field. Its Escape key
  stays in the field (first clearing it, then closing it), so it never closes a menu or a dialog it
  sits in. Give `HeadingSearch` a `label` that says what it searches, such as "Search wells".
- The heading reflows down to 320px without horizontal scrolling (WCAG 1.4.10).
