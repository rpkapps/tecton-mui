Add `Menubar`, a desktop-style application menu bar (the Material UI "Menubar" recipe, packaged as
components). `@tecton-mui/ui/Menubar` exports `Menubar` and its parts `MenubarMenu`,
`MenubarTrigger`, `MenubarPortal`, `MenubarPositioner`, `MenubarPopup`, `MenubarItem`,
`MenubarSubmenu`, `MenubarSubmenuTrigger`, `MenubarCheckboxItem`, `MenubarRadioGroup`,
`MenubarRadioItem`, `MenubarGroup`, `MenubarGroupLabel` and `MenubarSeparator`, plus
`menubarClasses`; all are also root exports. Behavior comes from Base UI (keyboard roving across the
bar, submenus, typeahead, focus return, RTL from the theme direction); items and popups use the
theme's `MenuItem` and `Menu` styles.
